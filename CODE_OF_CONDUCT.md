# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, focus on the work, and accept that
maintainers make the final call on scope. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are
not tolerated anywhere in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions).

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via the contact in `LICENSE` (Required Notice line).
Reports are handled privately and in confidence.
