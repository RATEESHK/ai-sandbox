# Trademark policy

The code of ai-sandbox is open source under the Apache License 2.0. **The names are not.** Section 6 of the
Apache License grants no rights to the licensor's trade names, trademarks or product names, and this policy
explains how they may be used.

"ai-sandbox", "aisb" and any ai-sandbox logo (the "Marks") identify the canonical project maintained by
Rateesh Koganti at https://github.com/RATEESHK/ai-sandbox and its official releases.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your product or setup **uses**, **works with** or **is based on** ai-sandbox
  ("built on ai-sandbox", "compatible with aisb policies").
- Writing articles, tutorials, talks, videos and reviews about ai-sandbox.
- Distributing **unmodified** official releases under their original names, with `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.
- Linking to the project.

## Needs written permission

- Naming a fork, a modified version, a product, a service, a company or a domain with a Mark or with something
  confusingly similar (for example "aisb-pro", "ai-sandbox cloud", "aisandbox.io").
- Offering a hosted or managed service under a Mark.
- Using a Mark or the logo in a way that suggests the maintainer endorses, sponsors or supports you.
- Selling merchandise with the Marks.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the Apache License, but a fork that is distributed to others must use **its own name
and logo**, keep `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` (which credit the original author), and state that it is derived from
ai-sandbox.

To ask for permission, open an issue titled **"Trademark request"** or contact the maintainer via the URL in
`NOTICE`.

*Trademark rights in some countries depend on registration and use. This policy states how the maintainer
licenses the Marks; it is not legal advice.*
